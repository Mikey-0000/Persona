# Protagonist cards: design spec

Roles: the three attached portraits are the PRIMARY visual and mood reference for each game's protagonist card (user-supplied assets, used as-is, cropped by CSS only).

## P3 (cool, ink-wash)
- Surface: white / very pale blue (#f4f7ff), no heavy fills. Ink navy #1b1d25 text, mid blue #5a6999, light blue #c1c9db hairlines.
- Signature: thin single-line curves (the headphone cable) drifting across the card; hairline borders; soft blue wash behind portrait; tall light caps with wide tracking.

## P4 (flat yellow, pop)
- Surface: acid yellow #efe30f-ish with olive #97995c concentric rings behind; black #0a0909 ink; cream skin tone #f2dca7 as portrait tint.
- Signature: olive ring/oval motif, houndstooth strip (black/grey #bab7ac) with gold roman-numeral tab, TV-stripe colour ticks.

## P5 (red, glossy, cut)
- Surface: red #d90019, black #0e0d0e angular cut, white text; glossy specular highlights (small white 4-point sparkles and soft radial dots).
- Signature: hard diagonal clip, black drop shadows, italic heavy caps, sparkles.
